An alt ctrl game about near dystopic future where groceries are awfully expensive.

**Players spin the wheel to throw money at the groceries they need to buy.** You can't really play the game without our
controller, so this page is more of a project showcase and doesn't include a game build.

**Stop the wheel when they reach the item's price** (or they'll overpay for it).

The theme of the jam was "mask", in Spanish that translates to "máscara", which if splitted (más-cara) means more
expensive.
